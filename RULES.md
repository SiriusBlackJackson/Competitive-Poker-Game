# Zheng Shang You — Rules (English)

Zheng Shang You (争上游, "fight to the top") is a three-player climbing card game
played with one 54-card deck. All three players play for themselves — there is no
landlord and no team. Five rounds are played; every round produces a 1st, 2nd and
3rd place.

## 1. Card strength

Low to high:

```
4 < 6 < 7 < 9 < 10 < J < Q < K < A < 2 < 3 < Small Joker < Big Joker < 8 < 5
```

So **5 is the strongest**, then 8, Big Joker, Small Joker, 3, 2, A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 7, 6.
4 is the weakest. (This is the traditional ranking of the region the game comes from,
which is why 5 and 8 outrank the jokers.)

The same order is shown at the bottom of the title screen in every language.

## 2. Allowed plays

1. **Single** — one card.
2. **Pair** — two cards of the same rank. Jokers never form a pair.
3. **Triple + 1** — three cards of the same rank plus any single card (4 cards total).
   Only the rank of the triple is compared; the kicker never counts.
   If your last three cards share a rank you may play them together as a **Triple**.

There is **no** triple + 2, no bomb, no airplane, no consecutive pairs, no straight,
no flush.

A play can only beat another play of the **same type and same card count** with a
**higher rank**. Otherwise you must pass.

## 3. Deck and seating

54 cards, 3 players, 18 cards each, free-for-all.

Turn order: **You → CPU A → CPU B → You**, repeating. All played cards go to the
centre of the table, where the last play and who made it stay visible.

When both opponents pass, the last player who played leads again with any legal play.

## 4. Round 1 — the 4 of Hearts decides who leads

The player holding the **4 of Hearts** leads round 1, and that first play **must
contain the 4 of Hearts** — a single 4♥, a pair that contains it, or a triple + 1
that contains it are all fine.

From round 2 on, the previous round's 1st place leads.

## 5. Places

- First to empty the hand = **1st** (上游)
- Second to empty the hand = **2nd** (中游)
- The remaining player = **3rd** (下游)

After the 1st place finishes, the other two keep playing until 2nd and 3rd are decided.

## 6. Trades from round 2 — two separate steps

Every round starts with a fresh deal, then the trades, then play.

### Step 1 — Tribute (mandatory, no refusals)

1. The previous round's **3rd place gives their single highest card to the 2nd place**.
2. The previous round's **2nd place gives their single highest card to the 1st place**.

The "highest card" is the highest card **in the hand before receiving anything**, so a
card that was just received is never passed on again.

### Step 2 — Request (optional card, may be refused up to 3 times)

3. The **2nd place may ask the 1st place for one card it needs** — to complete a pair
   or a triple + 1. The 1st place must actually hold that rank and may refuse; each
   refusal leads to a new request, up to **3 refusals**, after which a random card is
   given.
4. The **3rd place asks the 2nd place** the same way, with the same rules.

Restrictions: you may **not** request the giver's current highest card, and you may
**not** ask back the card that was just tributed.

## 7. Time

30 seconds per turn. Past 30 seconds you automatically **pass**; if it is your turn to
lead, a timeout plays your smallest legal lead instead.

## 8. Format

5 rounds. Every round's 1st / 2nd / 3rd is recorded, and a final scoreboard is shown
after round 5.
